Roadmap Snapshot Studio

A catalogue to pick from. Ticking copies an entry onto the roadmap you have open; from then on that row is its own — renaming it here never touches the other roadmap. P and D show where an entry has already been used.

    Columns

    A milestone is the green vertical line with a caption: a go-live that happens on its own date, independent of any initiative's delivery. Drag ⇥ Go-live from the toolbar onto the slide, or add one here.

    Read only. You can browse and export this roadmap, but not change it.

    click paints the selected phase · drag fills several sprints · Shift+click lays the whole sequence from that sprint on · Alt+click puts a second phase beside it in the same sprint · right-click erases · 1–4 pick a phase, 0 is the eraser. Title, sticky note, sprint headers and milestone captions are edited by typing on them; the green line drags between sprints and its ends stretch across rows. P presents full screen, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes, ? lists every shortcut.
